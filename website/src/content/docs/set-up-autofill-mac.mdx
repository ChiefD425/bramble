On a Mac, the Bramble desktop app fills your logins, 2FA codes and passkeys in Safari and other Mac apps through macOS AutoFill. It needs desktop app 0.20.0 or later.

## Turn on AutoFill

1. [Install the desktop app](/docs/install-desktop-app), open it and unlock your vault once, so AutoFill has your logins.
2. Open System Settings and go to **General → AutoFill & Passwords**. On older macOS versions, look for the same setting under **Passwords**.
3. Under **AutoFill from**, turn on **Bramble**.

To unlock AutoFill with Touch ID, open the desktop app's **Settings → Security** and turn on **Touch ID**. Otherwise AutoFill asks for your master password.

## Fill a login

1. In Safari or an app, click a username or password field.
2. Choose Bramble in the list that appears.
3. Unlock with Touch ID, or enter your master password and choose **Unlock**.
4. Pick the login to fill.

Bramble shows the logins for this site first. **Search logins** finds any other.

## Fill a 2FA code

On macOS 15 and later, click the code field on a site's 2FA page and pick the code from Bramble. It comes from the login's **Authenticator key (TOTP)**.

## Passkeys

On macOS 14 and later, when a site or app asks to create or use a passkey, choose Bramble in the window that opens. A new passkey works right away, and joins your vault the next time you open the desktop app. See [Passkeys](/docs/passkeys).

## Set up 2FA codes from a link

On macOS 15 and later, Bramble can take a site's 2FA setup link, so you don't copy the key by hand.

1. In **General → AutoFill & Passwords**, choose **Bramble** under **Set Up Codes In**.
2. On the site's 2FA setup page, click the setup link. Bramble opens, and once your vault is unlocked it shows **Add 2FA code**.
3. Choose **Existing login** and pick the login, or **New login**, then choose **Add** or **Save new login**.

If the site asks for a code to finish setting up, enter the one Bramble shows.

## Where AutoFill doesn't fill

- **Chrome, Firefox and other browsers** don't ask macOS for passwords or 2FA codes. Use the [Bramble browser extension](/docs/autofill-in-the-browser) there. Passkeys can still come from AutoFill.
- **Some apps** draw their own text fields and don't support macOS AutoFill, including many built with web technology.

## Questions

### Bramble doesn't show up in Safari?

Safari has its own switch for filling passwords, and Bramble needs it on. Some password managers ask you to turn it off so Safari stops offering Apple Passwords.

1. In Safari, choose **Safari → Settings** and click **AutoFill**.
2. Select **User names and passwords**.

Also check that **Bramble** is on under **AutoFill from** in **General → AutoFill & Passwords**.

### Why does Safari show Bramble instead of my logins?

On a Mac, Safari lists Bramble rather than each login. Choose it, and Bramble shows the logins for this site first.
