Bramble keeps your vault out of screenshots, screen recordings and assistants that read your screen, such as Gemini and Circle to Search. It's on from the start.

## Allow screenshots for a while

To save something as a picture, like a QR code:

1. Open **Settings → General**.
2. Under **Block screenshots**, choose **Allow for 10 minutes**.

Screenshots work until the time Bramble shows, then they're blocked again. Choose **Block now** to end it sooner. Quitting Bramble ends it too.

On iPhone, a blocked screenshot brings up **Screenshots are blocked**. Choose **Allow for 10 minutes** there, then take the screenshot again.

Your recovery code doesn't need a screenshot: choose **Copy** or **Download** where Bramble shows it.

## Turn it off

Turn off **Block screenshots** in **Settings → General**. Each device has its own setting.

If a screen magnifier or another accessibility tool shows Bramble as blank, turn it off.

## What each device blocks

- **Android:** screenshots, screen recordings, casting and assistants that read the screen. Android tells you the app doesn't allow screenshots. The autofill and passkey unlock screens are blocked too.
- **iPhone:** screenshots, screen recordings and screen mirroring. The app switcher shows a blank preview. If **Block screenshots** isn't in Settings, your version of iOS doesn't let Bramble block them.
- **Windows:** <Since commit="8ba4f1d3" on="desktop">screenshots, screen sharing and Recall.</Since><Until commit="8ba4f1d3" on="desktop">nothing yet.</Until>
- **Mac:** <Since commit="8ba4f1d3" on="desktop">Bramble asks macOS to leave its windows out of screenshots and screen sharing, but many current apps ignore that.</Since><Until commit="8ba4f1d3" on="desktop">nothing yet.</Until> On macOS 27 you can also keep Apple Intelligence out of Bramble: add it under **System Settings → Siri → Excluded Apps**.
- **Linux and the browser extension:** nothing. They give apps no way to block screenshots, so the setting isn't there.
